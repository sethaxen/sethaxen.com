Selected Projects

ArviZ: exploratory analysis of Bayesian models
Core contributor and governance member of the ArviZ project providing tools for exploratory analysis of Bayesian models. Lead developer of ArviZ.jl, MCMCDiagnosticTools.jl, PosteriorStats.jl, PSIS.jl and InferenceObjects.jl

CAGPJax
A JAX library providing scalable Gaussian-process inference that represents uncertainty arising from limited computation.

expax
A JAX library for highly parallel matrix-free computation of the action of the matrix exponential.

PollenClim
Building a consensus probabilistic model of paleoclimate from simulations of climate models and fossilized pollen data.

Pathfinder.jl
Accelerating Bayesian inference and early diagnostics for probabilistic models. I wrote this package both to accelerate my models and to test some methodological hypotheses.

Transforms
Working out and benchmarking efficient invertible transforms from unconstrained to constrained spaces for probabilistic programming. See Stan implementations for probability vectors and for orthogonal matrices.

JuliaManifolds
Co-author of ManifoldsBase.jl interface for defining manifolds and algorithms on those manifolds, along with implementations in Manifolds.jl. paper

ChainRules
Tools for defining and testing AD rules across the Julia AD ecosystem. Core contributor.

e3fp
3D-aware fingerprint representations of small molecules for machine learning. paper

Intro to Supervised learning
Interactive teaching resource on supervised learning, from linear regression to neural networks. Developed for the IntroML workshop series at the University of Tübingen.
